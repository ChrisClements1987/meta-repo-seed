## 🔄 Decision Process

### **Decision Types**
- **Strategic Direction**: Long-term vision and market positioning
- **Business Model**: Revenue, pricing, and go-to-market strategy
- **Market Strategy**: Target markets, competitive positioning, partnerships
- **Resource Allocation**: Budget, timeline, and resource decisions

### **Decision Criteria**
- **Alignment with Vision**: Does this support the "Business-in-a-Box" mission?
- **Market Opportunity**: Is there sufficient demand and revenue potential?
- **Technical Feasibility**: Can we implement this within our constraints?
- **Resource Requirements**: Do we have the necessary resources and timeline?
- **Risk Assessment**: What are the risks and how can we mitigate them?

### **Review Process**
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly assessment of all active decisions
- **Trigger Reviews**: When market conditions or technical constraints change
- **Annual Review**: Comprehensive review of all strategic decisions
- **Supersession**: When new decisions replace or modify existing ones
