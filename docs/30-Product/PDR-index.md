## 🔄 Decision Process

### **Decision Types**
- **User Experience**: UX design, user interface, user workflows
- **Feature Design**: Product features, functionality, capabilities
- **User Research**: User needs, market research, feedback integration
- **Product Strategy**: Product positioning, market approach, competitive strategy
- **Customer Experience**: Onboarding, support, success metrics

### **Decision Criteria**
- **User Value**: Does this provide clear value to users?
- **Market Fit**: Does this align with market needs and opportunities?
- **Business Impact**: How does this affect business goals and metrics?
- **Technical Feasibility**: Can we implement this within our constraints?
- **User Experience**: Does this improve or maintain good UX?

### **Review Process**
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly assessment of all active decisions
- **Trigger Reviews**: When user feedback or market conditions change
- **Annual Review**: Comprehensive review of all product decisions
- **Supersession**: When new decisions replace or modify existing ones

## 📝 PDR Template

When creating new PDRs, use this template:

```markdown
# PDR-XXX: [Title]

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Status**: [Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded]
**Review Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
[What user need or market opportunity is motivating this decision?]

## Decision
[What product change or feature are we proposing or implementing?]

## Consequences
[What becomes easier or more difficult for users? What are the business impacts?]

## Alternatives Considered
[What other product approaches were considered?]

## Implementation Notes
[How will this be implemented? Timeline, resources, success metrics]

## Review Date
[When should this be reviewed? Triggers for review]

---

**Decision Makers**: [Who made the decision]
**Consulted**: [Who was consulted]
**Informed**: [Who was informed]

*This PDR follows the Meta-Repo-Seed Product decision framework.*
```
