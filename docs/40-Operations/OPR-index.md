## 🔄 Decision Process

### **Decision Types**
- **Process Standards**: Development workflows, quality gates, procedures
- **Operational Procedures**: Deployment, monitoring, maintenance processes
- **Team Practices**: Collaboration, communication, meeting standards
- **Tool Selection**: Development tools, CI/CD, monitoring solutions

### **Decision Criteria**
- **Operational Efficiency**: Does this improve daily operations?
- **Quality Impact**: Does this maintain or improve quality standards?
- **Team Productivity**: Does this help the team work more effectively?
- **Maintainability**: Is this sustainable long-term?
- **Risk Management**: What are the operational risks and mitigations?

### **Review Process**
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly assessment of all active decisions
- **Trigger Reviews**: When operational conditions change
- **Annual Review**: Comprehensive review of all operational decisions
- **Supersession**: When new decisions replace or modify existing ones
