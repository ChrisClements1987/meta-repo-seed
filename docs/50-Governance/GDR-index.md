## 🔄 Decision Process

### **Decision Types**
- **Compliance Standards**: Regulatory compliance, industry standards
- **Risk Management**: Risk assessment, mitigation strategies, controls
- **Ethical Guidelines**: Ethical principles, responsible development
- **Policy Framework**: Organizational policies, procedures, standards
- **Audit Requirements**: Audit processes, reporting, accountability

### **Decision Criteria**
- **Compliance**: Does this meet regulatory and industry requirements?
- **Risk Mitigation**: Does this reduce or manage identified risks?
- **Ethical Alignment**: Does this align with our ethical principles?
- **Operational Impact**: How does this affect daily operations?
- **Long-term Sustainability**: Is this sustainable and maintainable?

### **Review Process**
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly assessment of all active decisions
- **Trigger Reviews**: When regulations or risk landscape changes
- **Annual Review**: Comprehensive review of all governance decisions
- **Supersession**: When new decisions replace or modify existing ones

## 📝 GDR Template

When creating new GDRs, use this template:

```markdown
# GDR-XXX: [Title]

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Status**: [Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded]
**Review Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
[What compliance, risk, or ethical issue is motivating this decision?]

## Decision
[What governance policy, procedure, or standard are we implementing?]

## Consequences
[What becomes easier or more difficult? What are the compliance and risk impacts?]

## Alternatives Considered
[What other governance approaches were considered?]

## Implementation Notes
[How will this be implemented? Timeline, resources, success metrics]

## Review Date
[When should this be reviewed? Triggers for review]

---

**Decision Makers**: [Who made the decision]
**Consulted**: [Who was consulted]
**Informed**: [Who was informed]

*This GDR follows the Meta-Repo-Seed Governance decision framework.*
```
