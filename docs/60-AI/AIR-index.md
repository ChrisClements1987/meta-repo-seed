## 🔄 Decision Process

### **Decision Types**
- **AI Integration**: How AI is integrated into development workflows
- **Agent Autonomy**: AI agent decision-making boundaries and capabilities
- **Safety Measures**: AI safety, ethical AI, responsible development
- **Prompt Engineering**: Prompt patterns, templates, and best practices
- **AI Governance**: AI oversight, monitoring, and evaluation

### **Decision Criteria**
- **Safety First**: Does this maintain or improve AI safety?
- **Ethical Alignment**: Does this align with ethical AI principles?
- **Effectiveness**: Does this improve AI performance and reliability?
- **Transparency**: Is the AI decision-making process transparent?
- **Human Oversight**: Does this maintain appropriate human control?

### **Review Process**
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly assessment of all active decisions
- **Trigger Reviews**: When AI capabilities or safety requirements change
- **Annual Review**: Comprehensive review of all AI decisions
- **Supersession**: When new decisions replace or modify existing ones

## 📝 AIR Template

When creating new AIRs, use this template:

```markdown
# AIR-XXX: [Title]

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Status**: [Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded]
**Review Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
[What AI capability, safety, or ethical issue is motivating this decision?]

## Decision
[What AI policy, capability, or safety measure are we implementing?]

## Consequences
[What becomes easier or more difficult? What are the AI safety and effectiveness impacts?]

## Alternatives Considered
[What other AI approaches were considered?]

## Implementation Notes
[How will this be implemented? Timeline, resources, success metrics]

## Review Date
[When should this be reviewed? Triggers for review]

---

**Decision Makers**: [Who made the decision]
**Consulted**: [Who was consulted]
**Informed**: [Who was informed]

*This AIR follows the Meta-Repo-Seed AI decision framework.*
```
